# G4 report: live recording waveform in Ideas

Key: `ideas-intents/G4/1` · Branch `ideas-intents` · Code commit: e6ac43057 (this report follows in its own commit)

## What changed
- **Page** (`ui/screens/ideas/init.luau`): while a memo is recording, the page shows `#ideas-voice` under Record. It is the composer's voice pill: `$zend.composer.voice.*` tokens, a pulsing dot, and `VoiceWave` from `ds/voice_wave`, unchanged. The phase maps as `starting` → breathing baseline and `recording` → `listening` (bars). The status line and its clock stay as they were.
- **Host** (`src/ideas.rs`):
  - The bars come from dictation's `Meter` (`src/dictation/meter.rs`, now `pub(crate)`): same 60 ms slots, 128 bars, −50…−12 dBFS scale and release.
  - The meter's input is the loudest RMS of what the recording takes. The real mic hands `zend_voice::Capture::start` the level atomic it already fills from the same callback that stores the samples. Before, it was created and then ignored. There is no second stream.
  - The test mic (`ZEND_IDEAS_TEST_MIC`) meters the WAV samples it plays in.
  - The bars are published as the shared `ideas_levels`, separate from `ideas`. They are cleared on Stop, Discard, `leave` and the end of a recording.
  - While recording, the loop waits until the next slot, as dictation does (at most 40 ms instead of 100 ms).
- `dictation.rs`: only `meter` and `levels_value` became `pub(crate)`. Dictation's behaviour is unchanged (its 14 tests pass).
